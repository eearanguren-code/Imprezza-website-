Questions

How long does an order take?

Most orders are ready 3 to 5 business days after you approve the proof. Need it sooner? Tell us your date and we'll tell you honestly whether we can make it.

Can I send my own photo or design?

Yes. Photos work best when they're sharp and well lit. For logos, a PDF, SVG or high-resolution PNG gives the cleanest result.

How do I pay?

After you approve your proof, we send a secure payment link and you pay by card. We start as soon as payment comes through.

Do you ship?

Yes, anywhere in the US. The shipping cost appears with your proof. Pickup in Houston is free.

Do you make orders for events and businesses?

Yes. Signs, glassware, tumblers and gifts with your logo, from 10 pieces. Include the quantity in your request and we'll quote a group price.
