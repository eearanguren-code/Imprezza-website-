Wall Printing
UV Direct-to-Surface Technology

High-resolution UV printing applied directly onto walls — drywall, concrete, brick, glass, and more. From feature walls to full-room murals, we print at any scale with stunning photo-realistic detail and fade-resistant UV inks.

Who We Are

BUILT ON
PRECISION

Imprezza is a Houston-based virtual custom printing and personalization studio. We started with a single wall printer and a belief that every surface — and every item — deserves to be extraordinary.

Today we operate across four disciplines: UV wall printing, DTF apparel, custom framing, and laser engraving. Our team brings together print engineers, designers, and craftspeople who care deeply about quality on every job.

We work with homeowners, interior designers, businesses, brands, event planners, and anyone who wants to make something truly their own.
